Rift guide

Rift is an operating system that runs from the drive it is installed on, and keeps your files on that drive rather than in the computer. This guide is on the drive too, so it is here whether or not the machine has a network.

  1. The drive What is on it, what a passphrase unlocks, and what happens to the computer.
  2. The desktop The bars, the menus, windows, the terminal, and the keys.
  3. Apps What comes with Rift, how to install more, and how to run one in a sandbox.
  4. Files, disks and printers Where your files are, what Rift does with the computer's own disks, printing and scanning.
  5. The assistant Asking questions on the machine itself, and searching your files by meaning.
  6. Snapshots, backups and a second drive Getting a file back, copying your home somewhere else, and cloning the drive.
  7. Updates How a new version is installed, and what happens when it does not start.
  8. Accessibility The screen reader, the on-screen keyboard and the theme.
  9. If something goes wrong What to check first, and where to report a problem.

Type rift guide in a terminal to open this guide again.
